Server operators

When you register a server we keep its name, your email address, the public URL if you gave one, the address you registered from, and when you accepted the terms. Your Push secret is stored only as a one-way hash. We use your email to confirm the registration and to contact you about the server. We do not share it.

App users

When the Beacon app turns on notifications for a server, it gives the relay a Firebase device token and gets back a handle that only that server can use. The server never sees the device token.

For each notification the relay keeps the sending server, the time, the device handle, the size and a fingerprint (hash) of the encrypted content, for 30 days. It cannot read the content: that is encrypted between the server and the app. Daily totals per server are kept for about a year.

Notifications travel through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on iPhone, Apple's push service.

Deleting data

Removing a server or signing out in the app deletes its handle. Removed handles are erased after 30 days. To have a registered server deleted, email abuse@meshcorebeacon.ca.
