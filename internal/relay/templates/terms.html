Terms for push servers

Version 1.

The MeshCore Beacon push relay is a free service of the MeshCore Beacon project. It delivers notifications from registered beacon-server instances to the Beacon app. By registering a server you agree that:

  1. You run, or are allowed to register, the server you name, and the email you give is yours and reachable.
  2. Your server sends only the alerts its users asked for, through an unmodified or compatible beacon-server.
  3. You do not try to reach devices that did not register with your server, get around rate limits, or send anything misleading.
  4. The relay records which server sent each notification, when, to which device handle and how large it was, and enforces rate limits. See the privacy notice.
  5. We may rate-limit, suspend or ban a server, and refuse new registrations from its email, if it breaks these terms or puts the service at risk.
  6. The service comes with no warranty and no promise of uptime. We may change these terms; a new version is shown to you before it applies.

Questions or reports: abuse@meshcorebeacon.ca.
